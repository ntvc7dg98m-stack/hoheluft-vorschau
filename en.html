Pediatric dentist in Hamburg-Hoheluft

Kinderzahnarztpraxis HoheLuft is a specialist practice for children's and adolescents' dentistry. We want every visit to be a positive experience for your child.

We speak German, English and Arabic.

You are welcome to bring someone along to help translate.

Book onlineCall us

Our treatments

  • Check-ups from the first tooth, at least every six months
  • Prophylaxis, professional cleaning and tooth-brushing school
  • Fissure sealing to protect against decay
  • Fillings and children's crowns
  • Treatment of chalky teeth (MIH)
  • Digital X-ray with low radiation
  • Laughing gas (nitrous oxide) for anxious children
  • Treatment under general anaesthesia

We treat children with statutory (public) and private health insurance.

How to book an appointment online

  1. Click the button below. The booking page (Dr. Flex) opens – it is in German.
  2. Choose the right appointment type – see the table below.
  3. Pick a date and time.
  4. Enter your child's name and your phone number, then confirm.
